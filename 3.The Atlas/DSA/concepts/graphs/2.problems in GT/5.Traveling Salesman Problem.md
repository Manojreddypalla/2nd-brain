## Brief

The **Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP)** asks:

> Find the **minimum-cost route** that visits **every city exactly once** and **returns to the starting city**.

It is one of the most famous optimization problems in Computer Science.

---

## Conditions

- ✅ Visit every city exactly once.
    
- ✅ Return to the starting city.
    
- ✅ Minimize the total travel cost/distance.
    

---

## Example

Suppose we have four cities:

```text
A ── B
│    │
C ── D
```

Possible tour:

```text
A → B → D → C → A
```

The goal is to find the tour with the **smallest total cost**.

---

## Time Complexity

- Brute Force: **O(n!)**
    
- Dynamic Programming (Held-Karp): **O(n² × 2ⁿ)**
    

TSP is an **NP-Hard** problem.

---

## Applications

- 🚚 Delivery route optimization
    
- ✈️ Airline route planning
    
- 🤖 Robot path planning
    
- 🏭 Manufacturing (PCB drilling, CNC machines)
    
- 📦 Logistics and supply chain
    

---

- **Branch and Bound**
    
- **Nearest Neighbor** (Approximation)
    
- **Christofides Algorithm** (Approximation)
    
- **Genetic Algorithm** (Heuristic)
    
- **Simulated Annealing** (Heuristic)
    
- **Ant Colony Optimization (ACO)**
    

---

## Memory Trick

> **Visit every city once → Return home → Minimum total cost.**

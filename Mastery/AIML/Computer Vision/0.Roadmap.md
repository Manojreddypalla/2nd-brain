# Final Mastery Checklist

By the end, you should be able to:

- Build classical vision algorithms from scratch.
    
- Train CNNs and Vision Transformers.
    
- Design and optimize object detection, segmentation, tracking, OCR, and pose estimation systems.
    
- Understand camera calibration, geometry, and 3D vision.
    
- Build multimodal vision-language applications.
    
- Optimize models for edge devices and GPUs.
    
- Deploy production-grade inference pipelines with monitoring and MLOps.
    
- Read, reproduce, and improve state-of-the-art research papers.
    

This roadmap is intentionally comprehensive and aligns with both enduring computer vision fundamentals and current industry directions, including transformer-based vision, multimodal AI, and deployment-focused engineering. It should remain useful for years because it is organized around foundational concepts first and evolving technologies later, rather than around specific versions of libraries or models.

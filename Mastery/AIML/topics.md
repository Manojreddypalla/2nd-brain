## First, understand what an AI Engineer actually does

Many people imagine AI engineers spending all day training huge neural networks.

In reality, most AI engineers spend their time building products that use AI.

Think of it like this:

```
User   │Frontend   │Backend API   │AI Model (GPT/Llama/Gemini)   │Database   │Vector Database   │Cloud
```

An AI Engineer connects all these pieces into a working application.

Only a small percentage of jobs require inventing new models.

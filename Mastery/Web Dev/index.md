## One addition I'd strongly recommend

Since your goal is to be **interview-ready as fast as possible**, I'd expand this beyond just revision notes. For **every chapter**, we should use the same structure:

1. **Core intuition** (why the concept exists)
    
2. **Deep explanation** (how it works internally)
    
3. **Mental model / visualization**
    
4. **Practical examples**
    
5. **Code walkthroughs**
    
6. **Common interview questions**
    
7. **Real-world usage**
    
8. **Common mistakes**
    
9. **How it connects to other technologies**
    
10. **30-second revision summary**
    
11. **One-page cheat sheet**
    
12. **Project integration** (where you'd use it in a MERN/Astro app)
    

That way, the book doubles as both a learning resource and a last-minute interview revision guide.

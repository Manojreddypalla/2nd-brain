## What to prioritize

For most software engineering, backend, DevOps, and AI roles, the highest return comes from mastering these in depth:

1. **PostgreSQL** (your primary relational database)
    
2. **Redis** (caching and messaging)
    
3. **MongoDB** (document database)
    
4. **Elasticsearch/OpenSearch** (search and log analytics)
    
5. **Kafka + PostgreSQL integration** (event-driven systems)
    
6. **pgvector or Milvus** (AI/vector search)
    

Once those are solid, learning Cassandra, Neo4j, ClickHouse, and other specialized databases becomes much easier because you'll already understand the underlying patterns and trade-offs.

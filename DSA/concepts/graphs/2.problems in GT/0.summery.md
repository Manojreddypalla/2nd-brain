# 📚 Graph Theory Cheat Sheet

## 1. Shortest Path Problem 🚗

**Question:** What's the cheapest/shortest route from A to B?

**Mental Model:** Google Maps.

**Algorithms**

- Dijkstra
- Bellman-Ford
- Floyd-Warshall
- A*

---

## 2. Connectivity 🔗

**Question:** Is everything connected?

**Mental Model:** Can every island be reached?

**Algorithms**

- DFS
- BFS
- Union-Find (DSU)

---

## 3. Negative Cycle 🔻

**Question:** Can I keep looping forever and make the cost smaller?

**Mental Model:** Infinite money glitch 💰

**Algorithms**

- Bellman-Ford
- Floyd-Warshall

---

## 4. Strongly Connected Components (SCC) 🔄

**Question:** Which groups can all reach each other?

**Mental Model:** Friend group where everyone can visit everyone.

**Algorithms**

- Kosaraju
- Tarjan

---

## 5. Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) 🧳

**Question:** Visit every city once and come back with minimum cost.

**Mental Model:** Delivery person planning the perfect route.

**Algorithms**

- Held-Karp (DP)
- Branch & Bound
- Approximation Algorithms

---

## 6. Bridges 🌉

**Question:** Which edge is the only road connecting two regions?

**Mental Model:** Destroy the bridge → city splits.

**Algorithms**

- Tarjan's Bridge Algorithm

---

## 7. Articulation Points 🚦

**Question:** Which node is the only gateway connecting regions?

**Mental Model:** Remove the junction → traffic stops.

**Algorithms**

- Tarjan's Articulation Point Algorithm

---

## 8. Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) 🌳

**Question:** Connect every node with the minimum total cost.

**Mental Model:** Cheapest internet cables connecting every city.

**Rules**

- Connect all nodes
- No cycles
- Exactly **V−1** edges

**Algorithms**

- Kruskal
- Prim
- Borůvka

---

## 9. Network Flow (Maximum Flow) 🚰

**Question:** What's the maximum amount that can flow from Source to Sink?

**Mental Model:** Water through pipes / Data through routers.

**Algorithms**

- Ford-Fulkerson
- Edmonds-Karp
- Dinic

---

# Shortest Remaining Time First (SRTF)

## Definition

**Shortest Remaining Time First (SRTF)** is the **preemptive version of SJF**. The process with the **smallest remaining Burst Time (RBT)** is selected for execution.

If a new process arrives with a **shorter remaining Burst Time** than the currently running process, the CPU is immediately taken from the current process and assigned to the new process.

---

## How SRTF Works

1. The scheduler selects the process with the **smallest Remaining Burst Time**.
2. If a new process arrives with a shorter Remaining Burst Time, the current process is **preempted**.
3. The preempted process returns to the Ready Queue.
4. This continues until all processes finish.

---

## Characteristics

- **Preemptive** scheduling algorithm.
- Selection is based on **Remaining Burst Time (RBT)**.
- A running process can be interrupted.
- Produces the **minimum average Waiting Time** among preemptive scheduling algorithms.

---

## Advantages

- Very low average Waiting Time.
- Better Turnaround Time than FCFS and SJF.
- Short processes finish quickly.
- Better CPU utilization.

---

## Disadvantages

- Requires knowledge (or estimation) of Burst Time.
- Frequent context switching increases overhead.
- Long processes may suffer from **Starvation**.
- More complex than SJF.

---

## Starvation

A long process may wait indefinitely if shorter processes keep arriving.

This problem is known as **Starvation**.

---

## Time Complexity

- Naive implementation: **O(n)** per scheduling decision.
- Efficient implementation (Priority Queue): **O(log n)**.

---

## Suitable For

- Time-sharing systems.
- Interactive systems.
- Systems where quick response is important.

---

## Not Suitable For

- Systems where Burst Time cannot be estimated.
- Systems where context-switch overhead is expensive.

---

## GATE Points ⭐

- SRTF stands for **Shortest Remaining Time First**.
- It is the **Preemptive version of SJF**.
- Selection is based on **Remaining Burst Time**.
- New shorter jobs can **preempt** the current process.
- Gives the **minimum average Waiting Time** among preemptive algorithms.
- Can cause **Starvation**.

---

# Example Problem

## Given

| Process | Arrival Time (AT) | Burst Time (BT) |
|---------|------------------:|----------------:|
| P1 | 0 | 8 |
| P2 | 1 | 4 |
| P3 | 2 | 2 |
| P4 | 3 | 1 |

---

## Step 1: Draw the Gantt Chart

- **0–1:** Only **P1** is available.
- **1 ms:** P2 arrives (BT = 4), which is shorter than P1's remaining time (7). **Preempt P1**.
- **1–2:** Run P2.
- **2 ms:** P3 arrives (BT = 2), which is shorter than P2's remaining time (3). **Preempt P2**.
- **2–4:** Run P3. (At 3 ms, P4 arrives with BT = 1. P3 also has 1 ms remaining, so continue P3 since it was already running.)
- **4–5:** Run P4.
- **5–8:** Resume P2.
- **8–15:** Resume P1.

### Gantt Chart

```text
0    1    2      4    5        8             15
|----|----|------|----|--------|--------------|
 P1    P2    P3     P4      P2          P1
```

---

## Step 2: Completion Time (CT)

| Process | CT |
|---------|---:|
| P1 | 15 |
| P2 | 8 |
| P3 | 4 |
| P4 | 5 |

---

## Step 3: Turnaround Time (TAT)

$$
TAT = CT - AT
$$

| Process | Calculation | TAT |
|---------|-------------|----:|
| P1 | 15 − 0 | 15 |
| P2 | 8 − 1 | 7 |
| P3 | 4 − 2 | 2 |
| P4 | 5 − 3 | 2 |

---

## Step 4: Waiting Time (WT)

$$
WT = TAT - BT
$$

| Process | Calculation | WT |
|---------|-------------|---:|
| P1 | 15 − 8 | 7 |
| P2 | 7 − 4 | 3 |
| P3 | 2 − 2 | 0 |
| P4 | 2 − 1 | 1 |

---

## Step 5: Response Time (RT)

Response Time = **First CPU Allocation − Arrival Time**

| Process | First Start | RT |
|---------|------------:|---:|
| P1 | 0 | 0 |
| P2 | 1 | 0 |
| P3 | 2 | 0 |
| P4 | 4 | 1 |

---

## Average Waiting Time

$$
\frac{7+3+0+1}{4}
=
2.75\text{ ms}
$$

---

## Average Turnaround Time

$$
\frac{15+7+2+2}{4}
=
6.5\text{ ms}
$$

---

# Quick Revision ⭐

- Type: **Preemptive**
- Selection: **Shortest Remaining Burst Time**
- Preemption: **Yes**
- Goal: **Minimum Average Waiting Time**
- Starvation: **Possible**
- Frequent Context Switching: **Yes**
- SRTF = **Preemptive SJF**

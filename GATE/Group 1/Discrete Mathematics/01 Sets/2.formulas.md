# 📐 Sets — Formula Sheet

> [!info]
> This note contains **all formulas, identities, and properties** related to **Sets** for quick GATE revision.

---

# Cardinality

The **cardinality** of a set is the number of elements it contains.

$$
|A| = \text{Number of elements in } A
$$

**Example**

$$
A=\{1,2,3\}
$$

$$
|A|=3
$$

---

# Power Set

If a set contains **n elements**, then the number of subsets is

$$
|P(A)|=2^n
$$

> [!example]
>
> If
>
> $$
> |A|=4
> $$
>
> then
>
> $$
> |P(A)|=2^4=16
> $$

---

# Cartesian Product

If

$$
|A|=m
$$

and

$$
|B|=n
$$

then

$$
|A\times B|=m\times n
$$

---

# Subset

$$
A\subseteq B
$$

Every element of **A** belongs to **B**.

---

# Proper Subset

$$
A\subset B
$$

Means

- A is a subset of B
- A is not equal to B

---

# Empty Set

The empty set is a subset of every set.

$$
\varnothing\subseteq A
$$

---

# Universal Set

Notation

$$
U
$$

Contains every element under discussion.

---

# Set Difference

$$
A-B
$$

Elements that belong to **A** but not to **B**.

---

# Complement

Complement of A

$$
A'
$$

or

$$
A^c
$$

Formula

$$
A'=U-A
$$

---

# Disjoint Sets

Two sets are disjoint if

$$
A\cap B=\varnothing
$$

---

# Number of Proper Subsets

If

$$
|A|=n
$$

then

$$
2^n-1
$$

> [!tip]
> Total subsets = $2^n$
>
> Proper subsets = $2^n-1$

---

# Set Identities

## Commutative Laws

$$
A\cup B=B\cup A
$$

$$
A\cap B=B\cap A
$$

---

## Associative Laws

$$
(A\cup B)\cup C=A\cup(B\cup C)
$$

$$
(A\cap B)\cap C=A\cap(B\cap C)
$$

---

## Distributive Laws

$$
A\cup(B\cap C)
=
(A\cup B)\cap(A\cup C)
$$

$$
A\cap(B\cup C)
=
(A\cap B)\cup(A\cap C)
$$

---

## Identity Laws

$$
A\cup\varnothing=A
$$

$$
A\cap U=A
$$

---

## Domination Laws

$$
A\cup U=U
$$

$$
A\cap\varnothing=\varnothing
$$

---

## Idempotent Laws

$$
A\cup A=A
$$

$$
A\cap A=A
$$

---

## Complement Laws

$$
A\cup A'=U
$$

$$
A\cap A'=\varnothing
$$

---

## Double Complement

$$
(A')'=A
$$

---

# De Morgan's Laws ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

$$
(A\cup B)'=A'\cap B'
$$

$$
(A\cap B)'=A'\cup B'
$$

> [!important]
> **De Morgan's Laws are among the most frequently used identities in GATE**, especially in Discrete Mathematics, Digital Logic, and Boolean Algebra.

---

# Important Facts

> [!note]

- Every set is a subset of itself.

$$
A\subseteq A
$$

- Empty set is a subset of every set.

$$
\varnothing\subseteq A
$$

- Order of elements does **not** matter.

- Duplicate elements are ignored.

- Cartesian Product produces **ordered pairs**.

$$
(a,b)\neq(b,a)
$$

# How we'll study together

Since I know how you like to learn, I won't just explain definitions.

For every topic we'll follow this format:

1. **Intuition** – Why does this concept exist?
    
2. **Real-world analogy** – Build a mental model.
    
3. **Formal concept** – The mathematical definition.
    
4. **Internal connections** – How it links to later topics.
    
5. **Examples** – Increasing difficulty.
    
6. **GATE PYQ pattern** – How it's tested.
    
7. **Revision notes** – Concise notes for your Obsidian vault.
    

This way, you're not memorizing Discrete Mathematics—you'll understand the structure behind it, which makes the later subjects like TOC, Algorithms, and Compiler Design much easier to connect.

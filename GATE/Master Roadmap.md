## One thing I'd change

I would **keep both systems**:

- **5 Groups** → Folder structure (organization)
    
- **3 Tracks** → Daily execution (what you study)
    

Think of it like software architecture:

```text
Vault (Folders)
│
├── Group 1
├── Group 2
├── Group 3
├── Group 4
└── Group 5

        ↑
        │ (Storage)

----------------------------

Daily Study
│
├── Track 1 (Career Core)
├── Track 2 (GATE)
└── Track 3 (Revision)

        ↑
        │ (Execution)
```

The **5 Groups** answer **"Where do my notes belong?"**

The **3 Tracks** answer **"What should I study today?"**

Keeping those two ideas separate will make your Obsidian vault much easier to maintain over the next several years.

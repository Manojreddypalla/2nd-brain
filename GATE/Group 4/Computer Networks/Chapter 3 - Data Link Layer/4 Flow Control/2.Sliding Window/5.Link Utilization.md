# Link Utilization

## Definition

**Link Utilization** is the fraction (or percentage) of time during which the communication link is actually carrying **useful data**.

In simple words,

> **It measures how efficiently the available bandwidth is being used for transmitting actual data.**

---

# Why Do We Measure Link Utilization?

Consider two situations.

### Case 1: Poor Utilization

```text
Send Frame

↓

Wait

↓

Wait

↓

ACK

↓

Send Again
```

Most of the time, the link remains idle.

---

### Case 2: High Utilization

```text
Send F0

Send F1

Send F2

Send F3

ACKs arrive

Continue Sending
```

The link is busy almost all the time.

Hence,

Higher utilization means

- Better bandwidth usage
- Higher throughput
- Faster communication

---

# General Formula

Link Utilization is

$$
\boxed{
U=\frac{\text{Useful Transmission Time}}
{\text{Total Communication Time}}
}
$$

where

- **Useful Transmission Time** = Time spent sending actual data.
- **Total Communication Time** = Entire cycle including waiting.

---

# Stop-and-Wait Utilization

We already derived

$$
U=\frac{T_t}{T_t+2T_p}
$$

where

- $T_t$ = Transmission Time
- $T_p$ = Propagation Delay

Notice something important.

The sender spends

```text
Transmit

↓

Wait

↓

Wait

↓

ACK
```

Most of the cycle is waiting.

Hence utilization is poor.

---

# Sliding Window Utilization

Suppose

Window Size = 4

Instead of sending only one frame,

the sender sends

```text
F0

F1

F2

F3
```

continuously.

The useful transmission time now becomes

$$
4T_t
$$

while propagation delay remains the same.

Hence

$$
U=
\frac{4T_t}
{T_t+2T_p}
$$

More generally,

if

Window Size

$$
=W
$$

then

$$
\boxed{
U=
\frac{WT_t}
{T_t+2T_p}
}
$$

---

# Using Propagation Ratio

Define

$$
a=\frac{T_p}{T_t}
$$

Substitute

$$
T_p=aT_t
$$

Then

$$
U=
\frac{W}
{1+2a}
$$

This is the most commonly used formula in GATE.

---

# Maximum Utilization

Sometimes

$$
\frac{W}{1+2a}>1
$$

But utilization can never exceed

$$
100\%
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{
U=\min\left(1,\frac{W}{1+2a}\right)
}
$$

or

$$
U=\min\left(100\%,\frac{100W}{1+2a}\right)
$$

---

# Why Does Increasing Window Size Improve Utilization?

Suppose

Window Size = 1

```text
Send

↓

Wait

↓

ACK
```

Most of the time is wasted.

---

Window Size = 4

```text
Send

Send

Send

Send

ACK
```

The waiting period is shared by four frames.

Hence,

effective utilization increases.

---

# Effect of Propagation Delay

Suppose

Transmission Time

$$
T_t=1ms
$$

Propagation Delay

$$
T_p=50ms
$$

Then

```text
Send for 1 ms

↓

Wait for 100 ms

↓

Send Again
```

Almost the entire communication time is spent waiting.

Utilization becomes very poor.

---

# Effect of Window Size

Suppose

Window Size

```text
1
```

Only one frame is in transit.

---

Increase Window Size

```text
8
```

Now eight frames are travelling simultaneously.

Idle time reduces significantly.

Hence utilization increases.

---

# Graphical Understanding

```

Large Window

```

More frames are transmitted during the same waiting period.

---

# Important Observations

Increasing

- Window Size increases utilization.

Increasing

- Propagation Delay decreases utilization.

Increasing

- Transmission Time increases utilization.

---

| Parameter | Effect on Utilization |
|-----------|----------------------|

| Propagation Delay ↑ | Utilization ↓ |
| Transmission Time ↑ | Utilization ↑ |

## One-Liner

> **Sliding Window improves link utilization by allowing multiple frames to share the same propagation delay, keeping the communication link busy for a larger fraction of time.**

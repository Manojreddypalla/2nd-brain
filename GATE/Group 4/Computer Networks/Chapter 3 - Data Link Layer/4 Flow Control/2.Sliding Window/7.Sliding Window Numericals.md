# Sliding Window Numericals

## Problem Solving Strategy

Whenever you see a Sliding Window question, identify these values first.

### Step 1

Write the given values.

- Number of sequence bits (n)
- Window Size (W)
- Bandwidth (B)
- Propagation Delay (Tp)
- Frame Size (L)

---

### Step 2

Decide what is being asked.

Usually one of these:

- Sequence Number Space
- Maximum Window Size
- Sender Window
- Receiver Window
- Link Utilization
- Bandwidth Delay Product
- Minimum Number of Bits
- Outstanding Frames

---

# Type 1 : Sequence Number Space

### Formula

$$
Sequence\ Space = 2^n
$$

---

### Example 1

A protocol uses

$$
n=4
$$

sequence bits.

Find the total number of sequence numbers.

Solution

$$
2^4=16
$$

Answer

**16 sequence numbers**

---

### Example 2

A protocol uses

$$
n=6
$$

Solution

$$
2^6=64
$$

Answer

**64 sequence numbers**

---

# Type 4 : Find Number of Sequence Bits

These are very common in GATE.

---

### Example 1 (Go-Back-N)

Maximum Sender Window

31

Find minimum sequence bits.

Solution

Formula

$$
2^n-1\ge31
$$

Add 1

$$
2^n\ge32
$$

Therefore

$$
n=5
$$

---

### Example 2 (Selective Repeat)

Maximum Window

16

Formula

$$
2^{n-1}\ge16
$$

$$
n-1=4
$$

$$
n=5
$$

Answer

**5 Bits**

# Type 5 : Link Utilization

Formula

$$
U=\frac{W}{1+2a}
$$

where

$$
a=\frac{T_p}{T_t}
$$

Remember

$$
U\le1
$$

---

### Example

Window Size

$$
W=4
$$

Propagation Ratio

$$
a=2
$$

Solution

$$
U=\frac4{1+4}
$$

$$
=\frac45
$$

$$
=0.8
$$

Answer

**80%**

---

### Example

Window Size

$$
6
$$

Propagation Ratio

$$
1
$$

Solution

$$
U=\frac6{3}
$$

$$
=2
$$

Impossible.

Maximum utilization

$$
100\%
$$

# Type 6 : Transmission Time

Formula

$$
T_t=\frac{Frame\ Size}{Bandwidth}
$$

---

### Example

Frame Size

1000 Bytes

Bandwidth

2 Mbps

Convert

$$
1000\times8=8000bits
$$

Transmission Time

$$
\frac{8000}{2\times10^6}
$$

$$
=0.004sec
$$

Answer

**4 ms**

---

# Type 7 : Propagation Ratio

Formula

$$
a=\frac{T_p}{T_t}
$$

---

### Example

Propagation Delay

$$
40ms
$$

Transmission Time

$$
10ms
$$

Solution

$$
a=\frac{40}{10}=4
$$

Answer

**4**

---

# Type 8 : Bandwidth Delay Product

Formula

$$
BDP=B\times T_p
$$

---

### Example

Bandwidth

10 Mbps

Propagation Delay

40 ms

Convert

$$
40ms=0.04s
$$

Then

$$
10\times10^6\times0.04
$$

$$
=400000bits
$$

Answer

**400 kb**

---

# Mixed Example 3

Given

Bandwidth

5 Mbps

Propagation Delay

20 ms

Frame Size

1000 Bytes

Window Size

10

Find utilization.

Solution

### Step 1

Transmission Time

$$
1000\times8=8000bits
$$

$$
T_t=\frac{8000}{5\times10^6}
$$

$$
=1.6ms
$$

---

### Step 2

Propagation Ratio

$$
a=\frac{20}{1.6}
$$

$$
=12.5
$$

---

### Step 3

Utilization

$$
U=\frac{10}{1+2(12.5)}
$$

$$
=\frac{10}{26}
$$

$$
=0.3846
$$

Answer

**38.46%**

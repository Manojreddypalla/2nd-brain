# Utilization Formula

## Total Cycle Time

One complete Stop-and-Wait cycle consists of:

- Frame Transmission Time = **Tt**
- Forward Propagation Delay = **Tp**
- ACK Transmission Time = **Tack**
- Backward Propagation Delay = **Tp**

Therefore,

$$
\text{Total Cycle Time} = T_t + 2T_p + T_{ack}
$$

---

## Utilization

Useful transmission time:

$$
T_t
$$

Total communication time:

$$
T_t + 2T_p + T_{ack}
$$

Hence,

$$
U=\frac{T_t}{T_t+2T_p+T_{ack}}
$$

---

## Simplified Formula

If ACK transmission time is negligible,

$$
U=\frac{T_t}{T_t+2T_p}
$$

---

## Using Propagation Ratio

Let

$$
a=\frac{T_p}{T_t}
$$

Substituting,

$$
U=\frac{1}{1+2a}
$$

---

## Important Observations

If

$$
T_p \uparrow
$$

then

$$
U \downarrow
$$

---

If

$$
T_t \uparrow
$$

then

$$
U \uparrow
$$

---

## Quick Revision

$$
U=\frac{\text{Useful Time}}{\text{Total Time}}
$$

For Stop-and-Wait,

$$
U=\frac{T_t}{T_t+2T_p}
$$

or

$$
U=\frac{1}{1+2a}
$$

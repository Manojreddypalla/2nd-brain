# Stop-and-Wait – Numericals

## Formula

If ACK transmission time is negligible,

$$
U=\frac{T_t}{T_t+2T_p}
$$

or

$$
U=\frac{1}{1+2a}
$$

where,

$$
a=\frac{T_p}{T_t}
$$

---

# Numerical 1

### Given

- Transmission Time ($T_t$) = **2 ms**
- Propagation Delay ($T_p$) = **8 ms**

**Find the Utilization.**

### Solution

Using,

$$
U=\frac{T_t}{T_t+2T_p}
$$

Substitute the values,

$$
U=\frac{2}{2+2(8)}
$$

$$
U=\frac{2}{18}
$$

$$
U=0.111
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{U=11.1\%}
$$

---

# Numerical 4

### Given

- Frame Size = **1000 Bytes**
- Bandwidth = **1 Mbps**
- Propagation Delay = **5 ms**

**Find the Utilization.**

### Step 1: Calculate Transmission Time

Frame Size

```text
1000 Bytes = 8000 bits
```

Transmission Time

$$
T_t=\frac{8000}{10^6}
$$

$$
T_t=8\ \text{ms}
$$

---

### Step 2: Apply the Formula

$$
U=\frac{8}{8+2(5)}
$$

$$
U=\frac{8}{18}
$$

$$
U=0.444
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{U=44.4\%}
$$

---

# Problem Solving Steps

1. Find the **Transmission Time ($T_t$)**.
2. Find the **Propagation Delay ($T_p$)**.
3. Ignore **ACK Transmission Time ($T_{ack}$)** if stated negligible.
4. Apply the utilization formula.
5. Convert the result into a percentage.

---

# Common GATE Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1

Using

$$
T_t+T_p
$$

✔ Correct:

$$
T_t+2T_p
$$

because propagation occurs in **both directions**:

- Sender → Receiver
- Receiver → Sender (ACK)

---

### ❌ Mistake 2

Forgetting to convert Bytes into Bits.

✔ Always remember:

```text
1 Byte = 8 bits
```

before calculating transmission time.

---

### ❌ Mistake 3

Confusing Transmission Time with Propagation Delay.

✔ **Transmission Time ($T_t$)**

> Time required to place all bits of the frame onto the communication link.

✔ **Propagation Delay ($T_p$)**

> Time required for the signal to travel from the sender to the receiver.

# Stop-and-Wait Efficiency

### What is Efficiency?

Efficiency tells us:

> How much of the total communication time is actually used for transmitting useful data.

---

## Why is Efficiency Low?

Suppose:

Transmission Time = 1 ms

Propagation Delay = 10 ms

Timeline

```text
Send Frame (1)

↓

Travel (10)

↓

ACK

↓

Travel Back (10)

↓

Next Frame
```

Most of the time is spent waiting.

The sender is idle.

---

# Total Cycle

```text
Useful Work

↓

Frame Transmission

+

Waiting Time

↓

ACK

↓

Propagation Delay
```

---

# Conclusion

Large waiting time

↓

Lower Efficiency

Small waiting time

↓

Higher Efficiency

---

# Important Facts

- Long-distance links reduce efficiency.
- Large propagation delay reduces efficiency.
- Larger frame sizes improve efficiency.

---

## One-Liner

> Efficiency is the fraction of total communication time used for transmitting useful data.

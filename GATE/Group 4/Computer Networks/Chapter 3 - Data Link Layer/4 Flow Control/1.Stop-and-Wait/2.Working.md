# Stop-and-Wait – Working

## Steps

### Step 1: Sender Transmits One Frame

```text
Sender  ------------->  Receiver

      Frame 1
```

The sender transmits one frame.

---

### Step 2: Sender Stops

After sending the frame, the sender **does not send another frame immediately**.

It waits for an acknowledgement.

```text
Frame 1 Sent

↓

Waiting...
```

---

### Step 3: Receiver Receives the Frame

The receiver checks whether the frame is received correctly.

If correct:

```text
Receiver

↓

Send ACK
```

---

### Step 4: ACK Travels Back

```text
Receiver

ACK -------------> Sender
```

The acknowledgement travels back to the sender.

---

### Step 5: Sender Receives ACK

After receiving the ACK:

```text
ACK Received

↓

Send Next Frame
```

The sender now transmits the next frame.

---

# Complete Flow

```text
Sender                     Receiver

Frame 1 ------------->

          <------------ ACK

Frame 2 ------------->

          <------------ ACK

Frame 3 ------------->

          <------------ ACK
```

Only one frame is outstanding at any instant.

---

# Important Facts

- One frame at a time.
- Wait after every transmission.
- ACK required.
- Reliable communication.

---

## One-Liner

> Send one frame → Wait for ACK → Send the next frame.

# 6. Service

## 🎯 Intuition

Imagine you're at a restaurant.

You want food.

Do you care **how** the chef cooks it?

❌ No.

You only care that the food reaches your table.

The chef provides a **service**.

---

## 🧠 Core Concept

A **Service** is **what one layer offers to the layer above it**.

The upper layer doesn't need to know **how** the lower layer works internally.

It only knows:

> "I need this functionality."

---

### Example

The **Transport Layer** provides this service to the Application Layer:

> "Give me data. I'll deliver it to the destination."

The application doesn't worry about:

- Retransmissions
- Error recovery
- Packet ordering

That's the transport layer's job.

---

## 🌍 Real-Life Example

When you book an Uber:

You ask for:

> "Take me to the airport."

You don't care:

- Which road?
- Which turns?
- Traffic decisions?

The driver provides the **service**.

---

## 🎯 GATE Point

**Service = What is provided.**

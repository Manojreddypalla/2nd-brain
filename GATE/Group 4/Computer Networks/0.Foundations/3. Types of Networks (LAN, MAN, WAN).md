# 3. Types of Networks (LAN, MAN, WAN)

## 🎯 Intuition

Not all networks are the same size.

A network connecting **2 computers in your room** is very different from the network connecting **India to the USA**.

So, networks are classified based on their **geographical coverage**.

---

## 🧠 Core Concept

### 🏠 LAN (Local Area Network)

A network covering a **small area**.

Examples:

- Home Wi-Fi
    
- School Lab
    
- College Campus Lab
    
- Office Floor
    

**Characteristics**

- High speed
    
- Low latency
    
- Usually owned by one organization
    
- Cheap to maintain
    

---

### 🏙️ MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A network covering an **entire city or metropolitan area**.

Examples:

- Connecting multiple college campuses in Hyderabad
    
- City-wide government network
    
- Cable TV network
    

**Characteristics**

- Larger than LAN
    
- Smaller than WAN
    
- Usually managed by ISPs or government
    

---

### 🌍 WAN (Wide Area Network)

A network covering **countries or continents**.

Examples:

- The Internet
    
- Bank networks
    
- Google's global network
    
- Microsoft's Azure
    

**Characteristics**

- Largest network
    
- Higher latency
    
- Uses public and private communication links
    

---

## 📊 Comparison

|Feature|LAN|MAN|WAN|
|---|---|---|---|
|Coverage|Building/Campus|City|Country/World|
|Speed|High|Medium|Lower (compared to LAN)|
|Latency|Low|Medium|High|
|Ownership|Individual/Organization|ISP/Government|Multiple Organizations|

---

## 🌍 Real-Life Example

Imagine you're uploading a file to Google Drive.

```text
Your Laptop
      │
   Home Wi-Fi      ← LAN
      │
      ▼
 Local ISP Network ← MAN
      │
      ▼
Internet Backbone  ← WAN
      │
      ▼
Google Data Center
```

One request can travel through **all three types of networks**.

---

## 🎯 GATE Focus

Remember the order:

```text
LAN < MAN < WAN
```

Questions often compare:

- Coverage
    
- Ownership
    
- Speed
    
- Latency
    

You usually won't be asked to memorize distances in kilometers.

---

## 💻 Developer Connection

When deploying a web application:

- Your laptop communicates over a **LAN**.
    
- Your ISP carries traffic through a **MAN**.
    
- Users worldwide access your server over a **WAN**.
    

---

## 📝 Revision

- **LAN** → Small area, fast, privately owned.
    
- **MAN** → City-wide network.
    
- **WAN** → Global network (e.g., the Internet).
    
- Classification is based on **geographical coverage**.

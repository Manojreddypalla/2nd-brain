# GATE Topic 3: Data vs Signal

This topic is **very important** because students often confuse **data** with **signal**.

---

# Intuition

Think of sending a letter.

- The **message written on the paper** is the **Data**.
    
- The **postman carrying the letter** is the **Signal**.
    

The receiver doesn't receive the message directly—the message is carried by something.

Similarly, in networking:

> **Data is the information. Signal is the physical representation of that information.**

---

# Definitions

### Data

> **Data is the information to be transmitted.**

Examples:

- Text
    
- Image
    
- Audio
    
- Video
    
- Files
    

---

### Signal

> **A signal is the physical/electrical/optical/electromagnetic representation of data used for transmission.**

Examples:

- Voltage pulse
    
- Light pulse
    
- Radio wave
    

---

# GATE Corner ⭐

## Definitions

**Data:** Information to be transmitted.

**Signal:** Physical representation of data used for transmission.

---

## Important Points

- Data is **logical information**.
    
- Signal is the **physical form** of data.
    
- **Signals travel** through the transmission medium, **not raw data**.
    
- Data is converted into signals before transmission.
    

---

## GATE Traps

❌ Data travels through the cable.  
✔ **Signals** travel through the cable.

❌ Signal is the information.  
✔ **Data** is the information; **signal** carries it.

# Physical Layer — Basic Signal Properties (Quick Revision)

These are the **4 fundamental properties of a signal**:

1. Amplitude (A)
2. Frequency (f)
3. Phase (φ)
4. Wavelength (λ)

---

# 1. Amplitude (A)

### Definition

The **maximum height (strength)** of a signal measured from the reference (zero) level.

### Unit

- Volt (V)
- Ampere (A) (sometimes)

### Formula

For a sine wave

$$
x(t)=A\sin(2\pi ft+\phi)
$$

where **A** is the amplitude.

### Key Points

- Determines signal strength
- Higher amplitude → Higher power
- Does **not** affect frequency

### Memory Trick

**A = Altitude**

→ Height of the wave

---

# 2. Frequency (f)

### Definition

The **number of complete cycles per second**.

### Unit

- Hertz (Hz)

### Formula

$$
f=\frac1T
$$

where **T** = Time Period

### Key Points

- Higher frequency → More cycles/sec
- Lower frequency → Fewer cycles/sec
- Inversely proportional to Time Period

### Memory Trick

**F = Fast Repetition**

→ Cycles per second

---

# 3. Phase (φ)

### Definition

The **starting position of a waveform** relative to a reference.

### Unit

- Degree (°)
- Radian (rad)

### Formula

$$
x(t)=A\sin(2\pi ft+\phi)
$$

where **φ** is the phase.

### Key Points

- Determines horizontal shift
- Does **not** change amplitude
- Does **not** change frequency

### Memory Trick

**P = Position**

→ Starting position of the wave

---

# 4. Wavelength (λ)

### Definition

The **distance between two consecutive identical points** of a wave.

(Crest-to-Crest or Trough-to-Trough)

### Unit

- Meter (m)

### Formula

$$
v=f\lambda
$$

or

$$
\lambda=\frac{v}{f}
$$

### Key Points

- Higher frequency → Smaller wavelength
- Lower frequency → Larger wavelength
- Frequency and wavelength are inversely proportional

### Memory Trick

**λ = Length**

→ Length of one complete wave

---

# GATE Quick Facts ⭐

- **Amplitude** → Height of the wave
- **Frequency** → Number of cycles/sec
- **Phase** → Starting position
- **Wavelength** → Length of one complete wave
- Frequency ↑ ⇒ Wavelength ↓
- Frequency ↓ ⇒ Wavelength ↑
- Amplitude affects **signal strength**, not frequency.
- Phase shifts the wave **left/right** without changing its shape.
- Wavelength is measured **crest-to-crest** or **trough-to-trough**.

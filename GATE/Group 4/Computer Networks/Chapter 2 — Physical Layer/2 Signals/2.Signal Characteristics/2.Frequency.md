# Topic: Frequency (GATE In-Depth Notes)

Frequency is one of the **three fundamental characteristics of a signal**.

The other two are:

- Amplitude (A)
- Phase (φ)

Frequency plays a crucial role in:

- Signal Transmission
- Bandwidth
- Nyquist Theorem
- Shannon Capacity
- Modulation
- Multiplexing (FDM)
- Wireless Communication

GATE frequently asks **conceptual and numerical questions** involving frequency.

# 2. Definition

**Frequency** is the **number of complete cycles (oscillations) of a signal occurring in one second.**

In simple words,

> **Frequency tells us how many times a signal repeats every second.**

---

# 3. One Complete Cycle

A sinusoidal signal

```text
Amplitude

 ^
 |      /\        /\
 |     /  \      /  \
 |____/____\____/____\____> Time
      ↑    ↑
    One Complete Cycle
```

One cycle consists of

- Positive half
- Negative half

---

# 5. Formula


::contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}


The relationship between **Frequency** and **Time Period** is

$$
f=\frac{1}{T}
$$

where

- **f** = Frequency (Hz)
- **T** = Time Period (seconds)

---

# 6. Time Period

The **Time Period (T)** is the time taken to complete **one full cycle**.

Example

If one cycle takes

```
0.5 second
```

Then

$$
f=\frac{1}{0.5}=2\ Hz
$$

Meaning

Two cycles occur every second.

# 12. Frequency vs Amplitude

Many students confuse these.

| Frequency | Amplitude |
|------------|-----------|
| Number of cycles/sec | Height of wave |
| Unit: Hz | Unit: Volt |
| Controls repetition | Controls signal strength |

Increasing amplitude

✔ Higher signal strength

❌ Frequency unchanged

Increasing frequency

✔ More cycles/sec

❌ Amplitude unchanged

---

# 13. Frequency and Wavelength

For electromagnetic waves,

$$
v=f\lambda
$$

where

- **v** = Wave Speed
- **f** = Frequency
- **λ** = Wavelength

As frequency increases,

Wavelength decreases.

This relation is heavily used in Computer Networks.

# GATE Corner ⭐

## Definition

**Frequency** is the **number of complete cycles occurring every second.**

---

## Formula

$$
f=\frac1T
$$

---

## Unit

```
Hertz (Hz)
```

---

## Related Formula

$$
v=f\lambda
$$

---

## Key Facts

- Frequency measures **how fast a signal repeats**.
- SI Unit = **Hertz (Hz)**.
- High frequency → More cycles/sec.
- Low frequency → Fewer cycles/sec.
- Frequency is inversely proportional to time period.
- Increasing frequency does **not** increase amplitude.

---

## Common GATE Traps

❌ Higher frequency means higher amplitude.

✔ Frequency and amplitude are independent.

---

❌ Frequency is measured in volts.

✔ Frequency is measured in **Hertz (Hz)**.

---

❌ Frequency and bandwidth are the same.

✔ Frequency is a property of a signal.

Bandwidth is a **range of frequencies**.

---

❌ Time period increases with frequency.

✔ Time period decreases as frequency increases.

# Memory Tricks 🧠

**F = Fast Repetition**

→ Number of cycles per second.

---

### Formula

**F comes First**

$$
f=\frac1T

### Difference

# One-Line Revision

> **Frequency is the number of complete cycles of a signal occurring every second. It is measured in Hertz (Hz) and is inversely proportional to the time period of the signal.**

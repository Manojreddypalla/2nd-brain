.# Topic: Wavelength (λ) (GATE In-Depth Notes)

Wavelength is one of the **fundamental properties of a wave**.

Along with:

- Amplitude (A)
- Frequency (f)
- Phase (φ)

Wavelength helps us understand:

- Signal Transmission
- Electromagnetic Waves
- Optical Fiber
- Radio Waves
- Microwaves
- Satellite Communication
- Nyquist & Shannon Concepts

GATE frequently asks **conceptual and numerical questions** involving wavelength.

---

# 1. Intuition

Imagine standing beside the sea.

```text
🌊      🌊      🌊      🌊
```

Notice that every wave has a crest (peak).

The distance from one crest to the next crest is always the same.

That distance is called the **Wavelength**.

---

Another example

Imagine a spring.

```
/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\
```

The distance between two identical points is the wavelength.

---

# 2. Definition

**Wavelength (λ)** is the **distance between two consecutive identical points of a wave**, such as:

- Crest to Crest
- Trough to Trough
- Compression to Compression
- Rarefaction to Rarefaction

In simple words,

> **Wavelength is the length of one complete wave cycle.**

# 6. Formula


::contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}


The relationship between **wave speed**, **frequency**, and **wavelength** is

$$
v=f\lambda
$$

where

- **v** = Wave Speed (m/s)
- **f** = Frequency (Hz)
- **λ** = Wavelength (m)

---

## Rearranged Forms

$$
\lambda=\frac{v}{f}
$$

$$
f=\frac{v}{\lambda}
$$

These formulas are very important for GATE.

---

# 7. Understanding the Formula

Suppose

Wave speed

```
300 m/s
```

Frequency

```
100 Hz
```

Then

$$
\lambda=\frac{300}{100}=3m
$$

Meaning

Each complete wave occupies

```
3 meters
```

---

# 8. Frequency vs Wavelength

This is one of the most important GATE concepts.

Assume

Wave speed is constant.

Then

If frequency increases

```
↑ Frequency

↓

↓ Wavelength
```

If frequency decreases

```
↓ Frequency

↓

↑ Wavelength
```

Therefore,

**Frequency and Wavelength are inversely proportional.**

# GATE Corner ⭐

## Definition

**Wavelength (λ)** is the **distance between two consecutive identical points of a wave**.

---

## Formula

$$
v=f\lambda
$$

---

## Rearranged Forms

$$

## Unit

```
Meter (m)
```

---

## Key Facts

- Wavelength is the **length of one complete wave**.
- Measured in **meters**.
- Symbol is **λ (Lambda)**.
- Frequency and wavelength are **inversely proportional**.
- Higher frequency means **shorter wavelength**.
- Lower frequency means **longer wavelength**.

---

## Common GATE Traps

❌ Higher frequency means larger wavelength.

✔ Higher frequency means **smaller wavelength**.

---

❌ Wavelength is measured in Hertz.

✔ Wavelength is measured in **meters**.

---

❌ Wavelength changes wave speed.

✔ Wave speed depends on the **medium**. Frequency and wavelength adjust to satisfy

$$
v=f\lambda
$$

---

❌ Distance between any two points is wavelength.

✔ Wavelength is the distance between **two consecutive identical points** (crest-to-crest, trough-to-trough, etc.).

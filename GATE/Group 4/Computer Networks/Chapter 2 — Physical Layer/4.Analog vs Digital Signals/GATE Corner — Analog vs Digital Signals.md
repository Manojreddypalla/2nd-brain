## 📖 Definitions

**Analog Signal:**  
A **continuous** signal that can take **infinitely many values**.

**Digital Signal:**  
A **discrete** signal that can take **finite (fixed) levels**, usually **0 and 1**.

---

## ⚡ Key Differences

|Analog Signal|Digital Signal|
|---|---|
|Continuous|Discrete|
|Infinite values|Finite values|
|Smooth waveform|Square/Pulse waveform|
|More noise sensitive|Less noise sensitive|
|Difficult to regenerate|Easy to regenerate|
|Lower reliability|Higher reliability|

---

## 📌 Important Facts

- **Analog = Continuous variation**
- **Digital = Fixed voltage levels**
- Digital signals are **regenerated** at repeaters.
- Analog signals are **amplified**, which also amplifies noise.
- Digital signals are preferred in computer networks due to **better noise immunity**.

# Characteristics of a Good Line Coding (GATE Quick Notes)

A good line coding scheme should have the following properties:

## 1. Self-Synchronization
- Helps the receiver identify the beginning and end of each bit.
- Achieved by having enough signal transitions.

---

## 2. No DC Component
- Average voltage should be close to **0 V**.
- Prevents baseline wandering.
- Suitable for transformer and capacitor coupling.

---

## 3. Efficient Bandwidth Usage
- Should require **less bandwidth**.
- Lower bandwidth → Better channel efficiency.

---

## 4. Error Detection Capability
- Should help detect transmission errors.
- Some coding schemes can identify invalid signal patterns.

---

## 5. Noise Immunity
- Should be less affected by noise and interference.
- Improves reliability of communication.

---

## 6. Simple Encoding and Decoding
- Easy to implement in hardware/software.
- Reduces complexity and cost.

---

## 7. Power Efficiency
- Consumes less transmission power.
- Important for long-distance communication.

---

## GATE Quick Facts ⭐

- **More transitions → Better synchronization**
- **Zero average voltage → No DC component**
- **Less bandwidth → More efficient**
- **High noise immunity → Lower BER**
- **Simple implementation → Lower cost**

| Encoding                    | Bandwidth      | Synchronization      | DC Component | Error Detection             | Advantages                                  | Disadvantages                               |
| --------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------- | ------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Unipolar NRZ**            | Low            | ❌ Poor               | High         | ❌ No                        | Simple, low bandwidth                       | DC component, poor synchronization          |
| **Unipolar RZ**             | High           | ✅ Better             | High         | ❌ No                        | Better synchronization                      | Higher bandwidth, DC component              |
| **Polar NRZ-L**             | Low            | ❌ Poor               | Low          | ❌ No                        | Simple, low DC                              | Long runs of same bits lose synchronization |
| **Polar NRZ-I**             | Low            | ✅ Better for long 1s | Low          | ❌ No                        | Handles long sequences of 1s better         | Long sequences of 0s still problematic      |
| **Polar RZ**                | High           | ✅ Good               | Low          | ❌ No                        | Better synchronization                      | Requires more bandwidth                     |
| **Manchester**              | High (~2× NRZ) | ⭐ Excellent          | None         | ❌ No                        | Self-clocking, no DC                        | High bandwidth                              |
| **Differential Manchester** | High (~2× NRZ) | ⭐ Excellent          | None         | ❌ No                        | Self-clocking, polarity inversion resistant | High bandwidth, more complex                |
| **AMI**                     | Medium         | ✅ Good               | None         | ✅ Yes (Violation Detection) | No DC, limited error detection              | Long sequence of 0s causes sync loss        |
| **Pseudoternary**           | Medium         | ✅ Good               | None         | ✅ Yes (Violation Detection) | No DC, limited error detection              | Long sequence of 1s causes sync loss        |

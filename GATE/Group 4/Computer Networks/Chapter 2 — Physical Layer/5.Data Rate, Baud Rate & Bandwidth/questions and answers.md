# Q1

### Problem

A communication system transmits **1200 symbols/sec**. Each symbol represents **1 bit**.

Find:

- Data Rate
- Baud Rate

### Solution

**Given**

- Baud Rate = 1200 Baud
- Bits/Symbol = 1

Formula

$$
\text{Data Rate}=\text{Baud Rate}\times\text{Bits/Symbol}
$$

Calculation

$$
=1200\times1=1200\text{ bps}
$$

### Answer

- **Baud Rate = 1200 Baud**
- **Data Rate = 1200 bps**

# Conceptual Questions

## Q21

**When are Data Rate and Baud Rate equal?**

### Answer

When

$$
M=2
$$

(Binary Signaling)

---

## Q22

**Can Baud Rate ever be greater than Data Rate?**

### Answer

No.

Because

$$
\text{Data Rate}=\text{Baud Rate}\times\log_2(M)
$$

Since

$$
\log_2(M)\ge1
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{Data Rate}\ge\text{Baud Rate}}
$$

Equality occurs only when **M = 2**.

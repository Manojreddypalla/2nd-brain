# GATE Corner — Guided vs Unguided Media

## 📖 Definitions

**Guided Media:**  
Signals travel through a **physical medium (cable)**.

**Unguided Media:**  
Signals travel through **free space (air/vacuum)** using electromagnetic waves.

---

## 📌 Types

### Guided

- Twisted Pair
    
- Coaxial Cable
    
- Optical Fiber
    

### Unguided

- Radio Waves
    
- Microwaves
    
- Infrared
    
- Satellite
    

---

## ⚡ Key Comparison

|Guided|Unguided|
|---|---|
|Wired|Wireless|
|Physical Path|Air/Vacuum|
|More Secure|Less Secure|
|Less Noise|More Noise|
|More Reliable|Less Reliable|
|Less Mobility|High Mobility|

---

## 🎯 Important Facts

- **Twisted Pair** → Cheapest, Electrical Signal, EMI affected
    
- **Coaxial** → Better shielding, Cable TV
    
- **Optical Fiber** → Light, Highest Bandwidth, No EMI, Long Distance
    
- **Radio Waves** → Omnidirectional
    
- **Microwaves** → Directional, **Line of Sight (LOS)**
    
- **Infrared** → Short Range, Cannot pass through walls
    
- **Satellite** → Large Coverage, High Latency
    

---

## ❌ GATE Traps

- Fiber → **Light**, not electricity.
    
- Fiber → **Immune to EMI**.
    
- Microwave → **Directional (LOS)**.
    
- Radio Waves → **Omnidirectional**.
    
- Infrared → **Cannot penetrate walls**.

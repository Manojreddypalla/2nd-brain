# GATE Corner — Transmission Media

## 📌 Definition (Must Remember)

**Transmission Media:**

> The communication channel that carries **signals** from the sender to the receiver.

---

## ⭐ Important Points

- Belongs to **OSI Physical Layer (Layer 1)**.
    
- **Carries signals**, not raw bits.
    
- Bits are converted into **electrical, optical, or electromagnetic signals** before transmission.
    
- Classified into only **two types**:
    
    - Guided (Wired)
        
    - Unguided (Wireless)
        

---

## 📖 Physical Layer Responsibilities

- Bit transmission
    
- Signal generation & reception
    
- Encoding
    
- Physical connection (cables, connectors)
    
- Voltage/Signal levels
    

---

## ❌ Physical Layer Does NOT Handle

- Routing
    
- Error Control
    
- Flow Control
    
- Addressing
    
- Packet forwarding
    

---

## 📡 Signal Used by Different Media

|Medium|Signal|
|---|---|
|Twisted Pair|Electrical|
|Coaxial Cable|Electrical|
|Optical Fiber|Light|
|Wi-Fi|Radio Waves|
|Bluetooth|Radio Waves|
|Satellite|Microwaves|

---

## 🎯 GATE Traps

- **Signals travel**, not bits.
    
- **Fiber → Light**, not electricity.
    
- **Wi-Fi → Unguided Media.**
    
- **Transmission Media ≠ Data Link Layer.**
    
- Physical Layer deals with **bits**, not frames or packets.

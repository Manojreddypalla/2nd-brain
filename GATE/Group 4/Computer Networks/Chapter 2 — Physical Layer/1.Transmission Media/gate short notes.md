# 📌 Transmission Media – GATE Short Notes

## Definition

**Transmission Media** is the communication channel through which data travels from the sender to the receiver.

---

# Types

```text
Transmission Media
│
├── Guided (Wired / Bounded)
│   ├── Twisted Pair
│   ├── Coaxial
│   └── Optical Fiber
│
└── Unguided (Wireless / Unbounded)
    ├── Radio Waves
    ├── Microwaves
    ├── Infrared
    └── Satellite
```

---

# Guided Media (Wired)

**Definition**

Signal travels through a **physical cable**.

### Advantages

- High Security
- Low Noise
- Reliable
- Stable Communication

### Disadvantages

- No Mobility
- Installation Cost
- Cable Maintenance

---

## Twisted Pair Cable

**Definition**

Two insulated copper wires twisted together to reduce **EMI** and **Crosstalk**.

### Types

### UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair)

- No Shield
- Cheap
- Easy Installation
- More Noise

### STP (Shielded Twisted Pair)

- Metallic Shield
- Less Noise
- Better Performance
- Expensive

### Applications

- Ethernet LAN
- Telephone Network

---

## Coaxial Cable

**Definition**

Copper cable having a central conductor surrounded by insulation and metallic shielding.

### Features

- Better Shielding
- Higher Bandwidth
- Longer Distance
- Less Signal Loss

### Applications

- Cable TV
- CCTV
- Broadband

---

## Optical Fiber

**Definition**

Uses **light** to transmit data.

### Principle

> **Total Internal Reflection (TIR)**

### Types

**Single Mode (SMF)**

- Laser
- Long Distance
- Very High Bandwidth

**Multi Mode (MMF)**

- LED
- Short Distance
- Lower Bandwidth

### Advantages

- Highest Bandwidth
- High Speed
- Long Distance
- Immune to EMI
- High Security

### Applications

- Internet Backbone
- FTTH
- Undersea Cable

---

# Unguided Media (Wireless)

**Definition**

Signal travels through **air or space** using electromagnetic waves.

### Advantages

- Mobility
- Large Coverage
- Easy Installation

### Disadvantages

- Less Secure
- Interference
- Weather Effects

---

## Radio Waves

### Characteristics

- Omnidirectional
- No Line-of-Sight Required
- Long Distance
- Low Frequency

### Applications

- Radio
- TV Broadcasting
- Bluetooth
- Wi-Fi

---

## Microwaves

### Characteristics

- Directional
- Line-of-Sight (LOS)
- High Bandwidth
- Long Distance

### Applications

- Cellular Networks
- Point-to-Point Links
- Satellite Communication

---

## Infrared (IR)

### Characteristics

- Short Range
- Line-of-Sight
- Cannot Penetrate Walls

### Applications

- TV Remote
- Wireless Mouse
- Keyboard

---

## Satellite Communication

### Definition

Uses an **artificial satellite** as a relay between Earth stations.

### Terms

- **Uplink:** Earth → Satellite
- **Downlink:** Satellite → Earth

### Characteristics

- Very Large Coverage
- High Latency
- Expensive

### Applications

- GPS
- DTH
- Weather Forecasting
- Global Communication

---

# Important Comparisons

## Guided vs Unguided

| Guided | Unguided |
|---------|----------|
| Wired | Wireless |
| Physical Cable | Air/Space |
| More Secure | Less Secure |
| Less Interference | More Interference |
| No Mobility | Mobility |

---

## UTP vs STP

| UTP | STP |
|-----|-----|
| No Shield | Shield Present |
| Cheap | Expensive |
| More Noise | Less Noise |

---

## Twisted Pair vs Coaxial vs Optical Fiber

| Feature | Twisted Pair | Coaxial | Optical Fiber |
|---------|--------------|----------|----------------|
| Medium | Copper | Copper | Glass |
| Signal | Electrical | Electrical | Light |
| Bandwidth | Low | Medium | Very High |
| Distance | Short | Medium | Long |
| EMI | High | Medium | None |

---

## Radio vs Microwave vs Infrared

| Radio | Microwave | Infrared |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Omni | Directional | Directional |
| No LOS | LOS | LOS |
| Long Range | Long Range | Short Range |
| Penetrates Walls | Poor | No |

# Formulas

## Wavelength

$$
\lambda=\frac{v}{f}
$$

where

- $\lambda$ = Wavelength (m)
- $v$ = Velocity of wave
- $f$ = Frequency (Hz)

---

## Frequency

$$
f=\frac{1}{T}
$$

---

## Time Period

$$
T=\frac{1}{f}
$$

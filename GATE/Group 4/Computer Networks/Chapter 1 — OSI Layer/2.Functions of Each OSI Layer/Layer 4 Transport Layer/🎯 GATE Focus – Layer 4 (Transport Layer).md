## ⚠ Common Mistakes

❌ TCP and IP are the same.

✔ TCP → **Transport Layer**

✔ IP → **Network Layer**

---

❌ UDP is always bad.

✔ UDP is preferred for **low-latency** applications such as gaming, VoIP, live streaming, and DNS.

---

❌ Port Number identifies a device.

✔ **IP Address** identifies the **host**.

✔ **Port Number** identifies the **application/process** running on that host.

---

❌ Flow Control and Congestion Control are the same.

✔ **Flow Control** → Prevents overwhelming the **receiver**.

✔ **Congestion Control** → Prevents overloading the **network**.

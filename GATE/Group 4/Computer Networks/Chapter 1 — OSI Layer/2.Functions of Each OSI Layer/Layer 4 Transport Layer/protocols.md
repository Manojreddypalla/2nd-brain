For **Layer 4 (Transport Layer)**, there are only a few major protocols. Unlike Layer 7, you don't have dozens to remember.

# 📌 Layer 4 – Transport Layer Protocols

|Protocol|Full Form|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|**TCP**|Transmission Control Protocol|Reliable, connection-oriented communication.|
|**UDP**|User Datagram Protocol|Fast, connectionless communication.|
|**SCTP**|Stream Control Transmission Protocol|Reliable transport with multi-streaming and multi-homing.|
|**DCCP**|Datagram Congestion Control Protocol|Unreliable transport with congestion control (multimedia applications).|

---

## ⭐ GATE Focus

Focus mainly on:

- ✅ **TCP**
    
- ✅ **UDP**
    

These two account for almost all GATE and interview questions.

---

## Quick Comparison

|Feature|TCP|UDP|
|---|---|---|
|Connection|Connection-Oriented|Connectionless|
|Reliability|✅ Yes|❌ No|
|Ordering|✅ Yes|❌ No|
|Speed|Slower|Faster|
|Error Recovery|✅ Yes|❌ No|
|Flow Control|✅ Yes|❌ No|
|Congestion Control|✅ Yes|❌ No|

---

## Common Applications

### TCP

- HTTP
    
- HTTPS
    
- FTP
    
- SSH
    
- SMTP
    
- POP3
    
- IMAP
    

### UDP

- DNS _(usually)_
    
- DHCP
    
- VoIP
    
- Video Streaming
    
- Online Gaming
    
- RTP
    

---

### 📝 Note

> **TCP and UDP are the two primary Transport Layer protocols. SCTP and DCCP are specialized protocols used in specific applications and are much less common in GATE questions.**

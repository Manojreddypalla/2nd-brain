## ⚠ Common Mistakes

❌ Session Layer provides reliable delivery.

✔ Reliable delivery is provided by the **Transport Layer (TCP).**

---

❌ Session Layer performs encryption.

✔ Encryption is handled by the **Presentation Layer.**

---

❌ Session Layer performs routing.

✔ Routing is performed by the **Network Layer.**

---

❌ Session Layer manages packets.

✔ It manages the **communication session (conversation)** between applications.

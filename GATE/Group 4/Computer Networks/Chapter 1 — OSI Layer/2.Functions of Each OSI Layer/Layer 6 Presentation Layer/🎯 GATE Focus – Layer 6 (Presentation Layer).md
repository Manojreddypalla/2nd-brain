## ⚠ Common Mistakes

❌ Presentation Layer transmits packets.

✔ It only **formats, encrypts, compresses, and translates** data.

---

❌ Presentation Layer performs routing.

✔ Routing is the responsibility of the **Network Layer**.

---

❌ Compression changes the meaning of data.

✔ Compression reduces the **size** of data without changing its meaning.

---

❌ Encryption belongs to the Transport Layer.

✔ In the **OSI model**, encryption is a **Presentation Layer** function.

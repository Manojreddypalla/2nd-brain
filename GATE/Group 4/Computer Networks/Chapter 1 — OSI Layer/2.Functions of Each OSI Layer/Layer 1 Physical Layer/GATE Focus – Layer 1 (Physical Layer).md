## ⚠ Common Mistakes

❌ Physical Layer uses MAC/IP addresses.

✔ It uses **no addressing**.

---

❌ Physical Layer performs framing.

✔ Framing belongs to the **Data Link Layer**.

---

❌ Hub is a Layer 2 device.

✔ **Hub and Repeater** are **Layer 1** devices.

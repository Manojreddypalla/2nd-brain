

## Chapter 1 — The Curious One

Chrome is known as the "weird kid."

While everyone else farms or hunts...

Chrome collects:

- broken circuit boards,
- strange books,
- old manuals,
- mysterious metal objects.

People laugh.

> "Those things are dead."

Chrome replies:

> "Maybe."

> "...but someone built them."

---

## Chapter 2 — The University

Chrome discovers a forgotten underground university.

Most rooms are destroyed.

One classroom still has power.

After restoring a small generator...

A handheld teaching device activates.

...

> **"Welcome, Student."**

The AI isn't surprised.

Its purpose has always been to teach.

---

## Chapter 3 — Learning

Chrome starts visiting every day.

The AI teaches:

- electricity,
- engineering,
- logic,
- computing,
- programming.

Not by giving answers.

By asking questions.

Chrome slowly realizes...

The books weren't myths.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Missile

One day...

The sky lights up.

An ancient missile launches.

Everyone panics.

Chrome immediately recognizes what it is.

Because...

The AI had shown historical videos.

Chrome had read about missiles.

But nobody believed the stories.

Hours later...

The missile hits.

A city disappears.

Silence.

---

## Chapter 5 — The Turning Point

Suddenly...

Everyone comes to Chrome.

Not because Chrome became a hero.

Because Chrome is the only person who even understands what happened.

They ask:

> "What do we do?"

Chrome honestly answers:

> "...I don't know."

Then...

The AI quietly says:

> "I do."

That's such a cool moment.

---

## Here's what I love most.

The villagers think:

> Chrome is a genius.

The player knows:

Chrome isn't a genius.

Chrome has **the greatest teacher humanity ever created**.

Chrome still has to build.

Experiment.

Fail.

Learn.

---

## I would make the AI feel ancient.

Not robotic.

Not ChatGPT.

More like...

An old professor.

Imagine:

Chrome:

> "Will we survive?"

AI:

(long silence)

> "..."

> "That depends."

> "Are you willing to learn?"

That kind of dialogue sticks with people.

---

## I would also hide the AI.

Only Chrome knows it exists.

Everyone else thinks:

> Chrome somehow knows impossible things.

That creates interesting tension.

Does Chrome reveal the AI?

Or protect it?

Because if people know the AI exists...

Some may want to control it.

Some may worship it.

Some may destroy it.

---

## The AI should have one rule.

It **never builds anything**.

It says:

> "I can tell you how."

> "I cannot do it for you."

That rule explains everything.

The AI preserves knowledge.

Humans create civilization.

---

# I think the emotional ending writes itself.

At the beginning...

The AI says:

> **"Welcome, Student."**

At the end...

After humanity has rebuilt schools...

Chrome becomes a teacher.

A child walks in.

Chrome smiles.

And says...

> **"Welcome, Student."**

The cycle begins again.

---

I honestly think **that** is a beautiful ending.

Because the story isn't really about AI.

It isn't even about computers.

It's about something much older than technology:

**Knowledge survives when one generation chooses to teach the next.**

That's a theme that could stay with players long after they finish the game.

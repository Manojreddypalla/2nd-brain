## Here's one thing I'd **avoid**.

Don't chase every meme.

Memes come and go.

Instead, learn the **timeless references**.

For example:

People still joke about:

- Windows vs Linux.
    
- Vim vs Emacs.
    
- "Hello, World."
    
- "Works on my machine."
    
- "Have you tried turning it off and on again?"
    

Those have lasted decades because they're tied to real experiences.

### One last thought

Don't try to become "a man of culture" by consuming everything.

Try to become someone who understands **why** certain things became cultural landmarks.

For example, don't just know that "It's always DNS" is a joke.

Understand that it's funny because countless network engineers have spent hours blaming complex systems, only to discover the problem was DNS all along.

That's the kind of understanding that lets you create your **own** jokes someday, instead of only repeating famous ones. And I think that's ultimately what will make _The Second Age_ feel like it has its own culture rather than borrowing everyone else's.
